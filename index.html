One eligible standard 18g single-bar price per supermarket in this snapshot: 45p + 35p + 34p. Their unweighted average is 38.00p; 38.00p ÷ 18g × 10 = £21.11 per kg. Calculations use the unrounded average. Sainsbury’s ↗ (checked September 2026); Tesco ↗ (checked September 2026); ASDA ↗ (checked September 2026). Multipacks, offers and prices outside the snapshot window are excluded.

Whole cooked lobster reference: Waitrose ↗, checked September 2026. Waitrose's displayed unit price for its frozen Canadian whole cooked lobster, 350g, on offer at £9.35 (was £12.50). Includes shell; not the price of edible meat alone. We use the retailer's stated £26.72/kg, which differs slightly from dividing the rounded pack price by its weight. Delivery is excluded. This offer was checked within 48 hours of the snapshot cut-off; current prices may differ.

Cadbury Dairy Milk 110g reference: Tesco ↗, checked September 2026. Tesco's public standard price was £1.85 for a 110g bar, with a displayed unit price of £16.82/kg. The separate £1.50 Clubcard offer is excluded. Online prices may vary by location. It was checked within 48 hours of the snapshot cut-off; current prices may differ.

Lithium reference: Daily Metal Price ↗, checked September 2026. Published GBP quote for 15 September 2026. The provider tracks lithium carbonate, not pure lithium metal. This is a commodity reference, not a retail purchase price. The earlier £15.31 quote was dated 14 September. What this quote measures ↗.

Bright copper wire (scrap) reference: Alveronix ↗, checked September 2026. Indicative scrap buying rate, subject to grading. A retail chocolate price versus a scrap buying price, for perspective; not a copper retail or exchange quote.

These are dated reference prices for perspective, not live feeds or interchangeable products. Eligibility was checked at the snapshot cut-off: commodity quotes within 30 days, retail prices and offers within 48 hours. Current prices may differ.

The method behind the madness

Same frog. Two very different prices.

We start with a 10p Freddo in 1995 and ask what it would cost if its price had risen in line with the UK Consumer Prices Index (CPI).

10p × comparison CPI ÷ 1995 CPI

Completed years use annual average CPI. For 2026, we use August 2026, the latest monthly figure in our saved snapshot—not a full-year average. Calculations use unrounded values; displayed comparisons are rounded to 0.1p.

Historical Freddo prices are approximate. The inflation line shows what a 10p Freddo would cost if its price had followed UK inflation.
