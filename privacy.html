Privacy & Cookies

We track Freddo prices, not you.

We’re here to track chocolate prices. Here’s how we handle information when you visit or get in touch.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Who to contact

The Freddo Index is an independently operated website. For questions about this notice, requests about your personal information or complaints, email thefreddoindex@gmail.com.

When you browse

You do not need an account to use the index. Our hosting provider processes technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver the website and protect it against misuse. Our purpose and legitimate interest is to provide a working, secure website.

The index loads fonts from Google Fonts and its chart library from jsDelivr. These providers receive information needed to deliver those resources, including your IP address and browser request details. Links to retailer websites only take you to those sites when you follow them; their own privacy notices apply.

When you get in touch

The contact form opens a draft in your own email app. It does not upload your name or message to a website database. You decide whether to send the email.

If you send it, we receive your email address, any name you supply, your message and any attachments in Gmail. We use these to respond, check reported prices and maintain the index. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in handling correspondence and improving the site. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and retain relevant evidence of price reports or follow-up discussions. We delete information when it is no longer needed. Sending a message does not automatically make it public; if we want to publish an identifiable quote or credit, we will ask your permission first.

Cookieless website analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views, understand which pages and referral sources are used, and measure loading performance so we can improve this website. It does not use analytics cookies or browser storage to identify visitors, and we do not use it for advertising or individual visitor profiles.

Analytics runs automatically unless you opt out. Use Analytics settings in the footer on either page to turn it off or on, free of charge. We rely on the UK statistical purposes exception for this limited website measurement, with clear information and a way to object. Where personal data is processed, our purpose and legitimate interest is understanding and improving the website.

Your browser sends technical requests to Cloudflare, including information needed to transmit them. Cloudflare provides aggregated traffic and performance reports. We do not send contact names, email addresses or message contents as analytics events. Read Cloudflare’s Web Analytics documentation and privacy policy for details.

Turning analytics off stops collection on subsequent page loads; measurements already sent or queued may finish during the reload and cannot be recalled. Counts are approximate: blockers, disabled JavaScript and opt-outs can prevent measurement.

Your saved preference and previous analytics

We store your analytics preference in this browser under freddo-analytics-opt-out-v2. This local storage is used only to remember your choice, not to track you. It lasts until you change it or clear browser storage. Clearing storage resets the preference. If we cannot read or save preferences, analytics stays off.

We honour saved rejections from our previous Google Analytics controls. Google Analytics no longer loads on this site, and we remove accessible old _ga cookies. Historical reports remain in our existing Google Analytics account; switching providers does not erase previously collected information. Its current retention settings are two months for event data and fourteen months for user data, with user retention reset by new activity. Standard aggregated reports are separate from those retention periods. Contact us about requests relating to earlier data.

Service providers and overseas processing

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare (analytics), Google (Gmail, Fonts and historical Analytics data) and jsDelivr help deliver these services. Providers may process information outside the UK. Where we make a restricted transfer, appropriate safeguards are required, such as UK adequacy regulations or approved contractual safeguards. Contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal information, restrict its use, or receive a portable copy. You can withdraw consent at any time.

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email thefreddoindex@gmail.com to exercise your rights. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when our use of information changes and show the update date above. Where a new purpose requires consent, we will ask before using optional analytics for that purpose.
